Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good skill match score?

A match score of 70% or higher is considered very strong for applying. Employers rarely expect a candidate to meet 100% of listed requirements. Even at 50-60%, highlighting your strong foundational learning ability makes you competitive!

How should I use the generated roadmap?

Focus on one phase at a time. Rather than just reading tutorials, build the suggested hands-on mini-projects. Having tangible code on GitHub is the single fastest way to prove skill competence to recruiters.

Does SkillBridge AI work without an internet connection?

Yes! SkillBridge AI includes an internal taxonomy of over 150+ tech skills, frameworks, and job roles. It analyzes requirements completely locally without requiring costly API keys or external dependencies.

Can I add custom or non-technical skills?

Absolutely. You can type any skill into the input box (e.g., Agile, Jira, Technical Writing, Leadership) and press Enter. The analyzer will categorize and cross-reference them accordingly.
